# A project must be realized by a group

We have to create something broadly related to the topic of aerospace engineering. 20-30 pages per project.

# Parts of the project

There might be a page on the platform that would include the project work/examples and stuff. The project needs to be in english from the beginning to the end.

# Parts

## 1.

> title
> students
> the course
> agenda
> introduction (1/2 pages), what is the project about.
> what is the aim/goal of the elaboration
> literature overview (any sources allowed, including books, papers, articles)

English/british, other pages as well. Translating via google/deepL allowed.

*It's good to use sources that are verified formally by institutions and scientists, not hobby-adjacent literature.*

Around 15 pages allowed.

*Diagrams and illustrations allowed and preferred to plain text.*

> conclusion of the previous info into a formulation of goals

# 2. 

> prepare & analyze, conduct research based on the sources and knowledge
> it's possible to use sophisticated tools, but also plain diagrams
