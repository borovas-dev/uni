## Preparation

Let's say we need to prepare a cobblestone. We need to establish such parameters:

- material,
- dimensions,
- shape.

There is a certain difference between conceptual drawings and real implementation.

The difference between ideal and real size = tolerance.

The tolerance is needed for:
- establishing production limits
- defining whether the object fits within established limits
